# Cross-sector climate-resilience overlay: evidence → cash conversion → price

**Added 18 November 2026. Method extension, not new certified research workflow.** It is compatible with the current `materiality`, `expectations`, `cross_sector`, `valuation` and `challenge` stages. Existing role prompts are amended so the normal workflow includes this overlay when physical adaptation is financially material.

## Taxonomy (no proprietary security universe)
Climate monitoring/early warning; cooling/HVAC; insurance/financing; resilient agriculture; resilient power; resilient urban infrastructure; water infrastructure. Use primary evidence to verify *issuer* exposure. These high-level labels are motivated by the user-provided Morgan Stanley climate resilience research (13 November 2026); **no bank stock screen, ratings or proprietary PDF is reproduced**.

## Research model
1. **Risk and need:** map georeferenced acute/chronic hazards, exposed physical assets, land and water rights, timing and alternative resilience options. Do not substitute headquarters for revenue or plant location.
2. **Bankability:** map ultimate payer; approved budget; procurement authority; cost-benefit; implementation capacity; financing currency/maturity; and supplier contract rights. Total adaptation needs are NOT signed orders.
3. **Corporate economics:** incremental annual revenue, recognized timing, contribution margin, fixed cost, maintenance/project capex, working capital, taxes, competition and replacement demand. Validate service mix and utilization.
4. **Valuation:** calculate marginal ROIC, incremental FCF, reverse DCF/expectations and the stock-price hurdle. Record as-of market timestamp and EV-to-equity bridge. Stop at NEEDS_DATA if unknown.
5. **Divergence:** compare a base, bear and upside case without invented probabilities. Avoid counting a single risk in both FCF and discount-rate premium.
6. **Prospective monitor:** freeze dated issuer operating hypotheses and falsification conditions before earnings or hazard observations.

## Illustrative peer studies (not stock recommendations)
- **HVAC:** TT, JCI, CARR — service mix, volume/pricing, cooling cost, adoption constraints and backlog-to-cash.
- **Resilient power:** GEV, HUBB, FLNC — regulated transmission spend, grants, lead times, warranty/augmentation, and overlapping AI load vs general electrification vs weather hardening.
- **Flood material:** HEI.DE — confirmed tenders, plant location/freight radius, segment price/mix and ROIC. **Separate adaptation materials revenue from existing Brevik CCS transition capital spending.**
